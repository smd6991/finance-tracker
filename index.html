Backup and restore

Your live data is stored locally in this browser. In v8.61, you can sync to your own Google Sheet, use Go Live Date for historical data, manage master data, personalize favorite reports/descriptions, and drag dashboard widgets/report pills into your preferred order. Export JSON for exact backup, or Excel for reporting and migration.

Excel import supports this app's exports and legacy workbooks that have a Records sheet with Date, Amount, Category/Subcategory, Source, and investment columns.
